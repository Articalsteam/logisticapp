Articals Studio platform console

The console runs inside the Articals Logistics app, not in a browser. It is locked to accounts that carry the platform administrator claim, which the backend grants from an allow-list — the app cannot grant it to itself.

  1. Install the administrative build: the Android admin flavour or the iOS ArticalsLogisticsAdmin scheme.
  2. Sign in with your Articals Studio address. It must be listed in the ARTICALS_BOOTSTRAP_ADMIN_EMAILS environment variable of the deployed functions before your first sign-in.
  3. Open the Platform tab. If it is hidden, your account does not carry the claim; ask another administrator to check the audit log.

Recording an offline payment or granting a billing bypass always requires a reason, and a bypass always has an expiry. Both are written to the ledger and the audit log — nothing in this console is anonymous, and nothing here collects card details.
